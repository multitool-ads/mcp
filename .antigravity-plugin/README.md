# Multitool Ads

[Multitool](https://multitools.pro) is an operational Meta Ads manager for media buyers who run
many ad accounts and Facebook profiles. This plugin connects your Multitool workspace to
Antigravity, so the agent can read and manage your ads from chat.

## After you add the plugin

Antigravity opens Multitool in your browser: sign in, choose a workspace and click Allow.

If the browser does not open, press `Ctrl+Shift+P` (`Cmd+Shift+P` on Mac), run
**Manage MCP Servers**, click **Authenticate** next to Multitool, sign in, then paste the code
shown in the browser into **Enter authorization code** and click **Submit**. In the Antigravity App
the same button is under **Customizations → Installed → MCP Servers**.

## What you can ask

- "Show today's spend and results across all my ad accounts"
- "Which campaigns spent more than $50 today with no results?"
- "Why is this ad set not delivering?"
- "Rank my creatives by cost per result over the last 7 days"
- "Pause every ad in campaign X" — the agent shows a preview and waits for your confirmation

## Safe by design

Every change to your ads is previewed first; nothing is sent to Meta until you confirm it. Multitool
only sees the ad accounts your connected Facebook profiles can access.

## Requirements

A Multitool account with your Facebook profiles connected — [multitools.pro](https://multitools.pro).
